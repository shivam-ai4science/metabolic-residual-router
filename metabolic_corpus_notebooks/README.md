# Metabolic Corpus Notebooks

The notebooks are ordered as follows:

1. `step-1.ipynb`: pathway and reaction acquisition.
2. `step-2.ipynb`: compound mapping and molecular processing.
3. `step-3.ipynb`: KO/gene/protein mapping and sequence acquisition.
4. `step_4_train_test_split.ipynb`: protein/ligand clustering and partition construction.
5. `step_5_negative_sampling.ipynb`: partition-matched non-edge sampling and final archive preparation.

They access KEGG, PubChem, and UniProt services and require compliance with the respective service terms. The committed copies have empty output cells; no scientific code cell was changed during collation.
